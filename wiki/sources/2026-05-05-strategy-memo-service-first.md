# Strategy memo "Service first", 5 May 2026

**Raw file:** `raw/alpstein/2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first.md`
**From:** [[lea-brunner]] (CEO) · **To:** Executive Board, Board of Directors
**Status:** Draft for discussion at the Executive Board meeting of 7 May 2026

## Summary

[[lea-brunner]] proposes that [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] move from robot manufacturer to operator partner ([[service-first-strategy]]). By 2028, service revenue should make up 50% of total revenue (today: about one third). Three initiatives support this: [[alpcare-plus]], [[alpmind-platform]] and [[agentic-ai-in-service]]. The memo names four risks: loss of focus, price pressure, liability for AI and personnel. Three decisions are requested from the Executive Board on 7 May 2026.

## Key points

- Hardware market for picking robots is getting tighter: three new providers from Germany and Poland entered Switzerland in 2025.
- Stated edge: not the robot but the operation – [[alpmind]] (fleet software) and [[alpcare]] (service).
- Headcount: **140 employees** (as of 5 May 2026).
- Goal: service revenue **50% of total revenue by 2028** (today: about one third, as of 5 May 2026).
- AlpCare stays at **CHF 1,200 per robot and month**; AlpCare Plus is calculated at **CHF 1,900** (proposal, 5 May 2026).
- Pilot budget Agentic AI: **CHF 180,000** (requested, 5 May 2026).

## Three initiatives

1. [[alpcare-plus]] – response time under 4 h, 24/7 remote monitoring, replacement robot within 24 h; target customers pharma and hospitals. Responsible: [[jonas-weber]].
2. [[alpmind-platform]] – AlpMind should also control robots from other manufacturers; first interface for a competitor's vehicles at [[rheintal-pharma-ag]]. Responsible: [[priya-raman]].
3. [[agentic-ai-in-service]] – AI agent classifies service tickets, resolves standard cases, hands complex cases to humans; goal under 10 minutes response time; pilot from August 2026. Responsible: [[jonas-weber]] with [[priya-raman]].

## Risks

- **Loss of focus:** AlpPick 2.0 in the fall pulls attention away from service ([[alppick-2-0-launch]]).
- **Price pressure:** customers compare AlpCare with cheaper pure maintenance contracts.
- **Liability for AI:** a wrong commitment by the agent makes Alpstein liable; clear rules needed.
- **Personnel:** service technicians are hard to find; [[remote-monitoring]] must make up for the shortage.

## Decisions requested (7 May 2026)

1. Approval of the target of 50% service revenue by 2028.
2. Approval of the pricing model AlpCare Plus.
3. Approval of the pilot budget for Agentic AI (CHF 180,000).

## People and organizations involved

[[lea-brunner]], [[jonas-weber]], [[priya-raman]], [[alpstein-robotics-ag]], [[rheintal-pharma-ag]].
