# Service first strategy 2026–2028

**Status (as of 5 May 2026):** Proposed – draft memo for the Executive Board meeting of 7 May 2026. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Description

Strategy of [[lea-brunner]]: [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] moves from robot manufacturer to operator partner. Customers buy "the assurance that their warehouse keeps running". (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Goal

- Service revenue **50% of total revenue by 2028** (today: about one third, as of 5 May 2026). (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Initiatives

- [[alpcare-plus]] – responsible [[jonas-weber]]
- [[alpmind-platform]] – responsible [[priya-raman]]

(Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
