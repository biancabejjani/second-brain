# AlpMind

Fleet software of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]]; provides [[remote-monitoring]] of the robots. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## Facts

- Remote monitoring via AlpMind reduces on-site visits by about 30% (reported by [[jonas-weber]], 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

- Should also control robots from other manufacturers → [[alpmind-platform]]. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Named, with AlpCare, as the company's edge over hardware competitors. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

- Source of service tickets for the triage agent in [[agentic-ai-in-service]]. (Source: [[2026-08-21-kickoff-notes-agentic-ai-pilot]])

- Software revenue Q2 2026: CHF 0.7 m (plan 0.6); licenses grow with the fleet. (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- Interface to third-party robots at prototype stage ([[alpmind-platform]], as of 30 July 2026). (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
