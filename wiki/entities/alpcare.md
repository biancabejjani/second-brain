# AlpCare

Service subscription of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]], billed per robot and month. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## Facts

- Price: **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Service revenue (AlpCare) is growing more slowly than planned (no figure given). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Customers are asking for a variant with a guaranteed response time under 4 hours → [[alpcare-plus]]. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Responsible: [[jonas-weber]] (Head of Service). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## Update 5 May 2026

- Price stays at **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 5 May 2026). (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Risk: customers compare AlpCare with pure maintenance contracts, which are significantly cheaper. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Part of the [[service-first-strategy]]: service share today about one third, target 50% by 2028. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Update 16 June 2026

- Jonas Weber to Bergland: "AlpCare now costs CHF 1,450 per robot and month" with guaranteed response time. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

> [!warning] Contradiction – AlpCare price
> CHF 1,200 per robot and month (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]) and (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]) vs. CHF 1,450 per robot and month (Jonas Weber to Bergland, 16 June 2026) (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). Sandra Koller writes that "the pricing question for AlpCare/AlpCare Plus has not been decided yet" (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). Not resolved.

## Update 30 July 2026

- Service revenue Q2 2026: **CHF 3.0 m** (plan 2.8); service share 31%. (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- 297 of 412 robots (72%) have an AlpCare contract (30 June 2026). (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- AlpCare price list (**CHF 1,200**) **unchanged** (as of 30 July 2026). (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])

> [!warning] Contradiction – AlpCare price (still open)
> The Q2 report confirms CHF 1,200 (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]]). Jonas Weber quoted CHF 1,450 to Bergland on 16 June 2026 (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). A third source for 1,200 does not explain the 1,450. Not resolved.
