# Remote monitoring

Monitoring of robots in the field from a distance via [[alpmind]], as part of the service [[alpcare]]. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## Facts

- Reduces on-site visits by about 30% (reported by [[jonas-weber]], 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

> [!note] Uncertain
> The minutes do not say how the 30% was measured or over which period.

- Must make up for the shortage of service technicians (risk "personnel"). (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- AlpCare Plus includes remote monitoring around the clock ([[alpcare-plus]]). (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

- Bergland, 15 June 2026: remote monitoring reported the error, but nobody was reachable before 10 a.m. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
