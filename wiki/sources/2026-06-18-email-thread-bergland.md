# Email thread Bergland Logistik AG, 16–18 June 2026

**Raw file:** `raw/alpstein/2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland.md`
**Export:** mailbox service@alpstein-robotics.example, 18 June 2026
**Participants:** [[thomas-rueegg]] ([[bergland-logistik-ag]]), [[jonas-weber]], [[sandra-koller]]; [[marco-steiner]] mentioned

## Summary

[[thomas-rueegg]] complains that four [[alppick]] robots in Hall 3 stood still for almost two hours – the third incident in four weeks – and asks whether the AlpPick 2.0 launch stays in September. [[jonas-weber]] names a navigation software error after the update of 20 May as the cause, quotes an AlpCare price of CHF 1,450 with guaranteed response time and says the launch is assumed for Q4, not yet confirmed. [[sandra-koller]] warns internally not to commit to anything before [[marco-steiner]] has seen it. Rüegg thanks Weber "for the commitment" and asks for a credit note; Weber tells Koller he did not commit to anything concrete.

## Key points

- 15 June 2026: four AlpPick robots in Hall 3 at Bergland stood still for almost two hours; third incident in four weeks. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- [[remote-monitoring]] reported the error, but nobody was reachable before 10 a.m. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Cost for Bergland: about **CHF 4,000 per hour of downtime** (statement by Rüegg, 16 June 2026). (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Cause according to Weber: error in the navigation software after the update of 20 May; patch in test phase, rollout "next week". (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Weber: "AlpCare now costs **CHF 1,450 per robot and month**" with guaranteed response time (16 June 2026). (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Weber: AlpPick 2.0 launch "we currently assume Q4. This is not yet confirmed" (16 June 2026). (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Koller: Bergland is the largest customer; a credit note sets a precedent; pricing AlpCare/AlpCare Plus not decided yet. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Rüegg: Q4 is a problem; Bergland planned its expansion for September. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
