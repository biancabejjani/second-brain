# Executive Board minutes, 12 March 2026

**Raw file:** `raw/alpstein/2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes.md`
**Date and place:** 12 March 2026, 08:30–10:15, Appenzell (Säntis meeting room)
**Chair:** [[lea-brunner]] · **Minutes:** Executive Board assistant (not named)

## Summary

The Executive Board of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] reviewed business performance for January–February: order intake is 12% above the previous year, driven by [[alppick]] among logistics customers, while service revenue ([[alpcare]]) is growing more slowly than planned. The board decided on the market launch of AlpPick 2.0 in September 2026 ([[alppick-2-0-launch]]). A premium service variant with a guaranteed response time ([[alpcare-plus]]) will be priced by the May meeting. Six software development positions are open ([[recruitment-software-development]]), and onboarding is to be digitized ([[digital-onboarding]]).

## Key points

- Order intake January–February 2026: +12% vs. previous year; driver: AlpPick among logistics customers.
- Service revenue (AlpCare) grows more slowly than planned (no figure given).
- Headcount: **120 employees** at Appenzell and Buchs SG (as of 12 March 2026).
- AlpPick 2.0: payload 25 kg instead of 15 kg, navigation without floor markings; two pilot robots at [[bergland-logistik-ag]] in Buchs since February 2026.
- AlpPick 2.0 open points: battery life in the cold store (target 8 h, currently 6.5 h); CE certification in progress.
- [[remote-monitoring]] via [[alpmind]] reduces on-site visits by about 30%.
- AlpCare price: **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 12 March 2026).
- Customers ask for a response time under 4 hours → proposal "AlpCare Plus" to be presented in May with the strategy memo.

## Decisions

1. No adjustment to the annual budget; next review in June 2026.
2. **Market launch of AlpPick 2.0 in September 2026.** [[sandra-koller]] prepares the customer communication by end of May.
3. [[jonas-weber]] and [[marco-steiner]] develop a pricing model for AlpCare Plus by the May meeting.
