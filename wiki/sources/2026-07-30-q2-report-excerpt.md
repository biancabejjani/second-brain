# Quarterly report Q2 2026 (excerpt), 30 July 2026

**Raw file:** `raw/alpstein/2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt.md`
**Reporting period:** 1 April – 30 June 2026 · **Prepared by:** Finance ([[marco-steiner]]) · **Date:** 30 July 2026 · **Confidentiality:** internal

## Summary

In Q2 2026, revenue of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] was slightly above plan (CHF 9.6 m vs. 9.4 m) and EBIT slightly below (CHF 0.9 m vs. 1.1 m). Reasons: higher service costs from three downtime incidents at [[bergland-logistik-ag]] and earlier-than-planned recruitment in software development. The service share of revenue rose to 31% ([[service-first-strategy]]). Headcount on 30 June 2026 is 140 (131 FTE). The full-year outlook remains CHF 38 m revenue and 10% EBIT margin.

## Commentary by area

- **Hardware:** 34 new AlpPick delivered – 18 to [[bergland-logistik-ag]] (expansion of Halls 3 and 4), 9 to [[toggenburg-moebel-ag]] (new customer). 41 pre-orders for AlpPick 2.0. Launch date to be set at the Executive Board meeting in August ([[alppick-2-0-launch]]). (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- **Software:** AlpMind licenses grow with the fleet. Interface to third-party robots ([[alpmind-platform]]) at prototype stage; first test at [[rheintal-pharma-ag]] planned for Q3. (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- **Service:** AlpCare contracts grow faster than the fleet. Three downtime incidents at Bergland (May/June) caused special costs of **CHF 62,000**. A credit note to Bergland has **not** been booked; the decision lies with the Executive Board. Pricing model [[alpcare-plus]] (CHF 1,900) **approved**; AlpCare price list (CHF 1,200) **unchanged**. (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- **Personnel:** 12 new hires, 8 of them in software development ([[recruitment-software-development]]). 4 open positions in service field staff still unfilled. (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])

## Risks and outlook

1. Delivery date AlpPick 2.0: a postponement to Q4 would endanger the pre-orders; Bergland planned an expansion for September. (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
2. Service costs: navigation error fixed since the patch of 24 June; further downtime would weigh on the margin. (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
3. Agentic AI pilot ([[agentic-ai-in-service]]): budget CHF 180,000 approved; kickoff in August; the risk lies in the rules – what may the agent commit to toward customers? (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- Full-year outlook: **CHF 38 m revenue, 10% EBIT margin** (as of 30 July 2026). (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
