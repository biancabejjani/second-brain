# Agentic AI in service

**Status (as of 5 May 2026):** Proposed – pilot from August 2026; budget CHF 180,000 requested. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Description

An AI agent classifies service tickets automatically, resolves standard cases directly and hands complex cases over to humans. Goal: response time under 10 minutes in the standard case. Initiative 3 of the [[service-first-strategy]]. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## People

- Responsible: [[jonas-weber]] with [[priya-raman]]. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Risks

- Liability: if the agent makes a wrong commitment in service, Alpstein is liable – clear rules for what the agent may do are needed. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Open points

- Approval of the pilot budget (CHF 180,000): requested for 7 May 2026, decision not yet in the wiki.
- Rules for what the agent may do: not yet defined.
